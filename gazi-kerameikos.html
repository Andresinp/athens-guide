Gazi, Kerameikos & Metaxourgeio

Former industrial blocks west of the centre: galleries, queer clubs, late bars and new bistros. The most 'alternative' zone.

Reviewers mention: pappardelle, greek food, bar seating, pork chops.

Seychelles (Σεϋχέλλες) is a modern-Greek sharing-plates restaurant with outdoor tables on a lively pedestrian square in Metaxourgeio/Kerameikos. Booking is recommended. An early Athens 'bistronomy' spot, it serves ingredient-driven Greek 'soul food' with rustic-meets-urban cooking and is popular with locals and tourists.

Gazi, Kerameikos & Metaxourgeio

Former industrial blocks west of the centre: galleries, queer clubs, late bars and new bistros. The most 'alternative' zone.

Vibe key: ● Alternative · ● Trendy · ● Classic · ● Posh · ● Must-see

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants

21Seychelles

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Alternativefrom Erhan & Irene
Keramikou 49, 104 36 Athens · Metaxourgeio · ~3.5 km (metro/taxi) from base

Yes, the pin is Seychelles (Σεϋχέλλες): an early Athens 'bistronomy' spot serving ingredient-driven Greek 'soul food' on a lively Metaxourgeio square; popular with locals and tourists, booking recommended.

☕ Cafés & brunch

22Beauty Killed the Beast

☕ Cafés & brunch● Alternativefrom Erhan
Paramythias 14, 104 35 Athens · Kerameikos / Metaxourgeio · ~3.5 km (metro/taxi) from base

All-day café-bar in a restored 1930s neoclassical mansion on the pedestrian part of Paramythias; brunch by day, DJs and cocktails at night, with a garden and King Kong art on the facade.

🪩 Clubs & late night

23SMUT Athens

🪩 Clubs & late night● Alternativefrom Erhan & Giulia
Vatsaxi 4, 104 38 Athens · Metaxourgeio / Plateia Vathis · ~2.8 km (metro/taxi) from base

Identified: Vatsaxi 4 is SMUT, an underground queer techno club open on Saturdays. It is known for its safe-space and no-camera policies and a raw, red-lit, Berlin-warehouse look.

🪩 Clubs & late night● Trendyfrom Erhan
Triptolemou 10, 118 54 Athens · Gazi · ~4.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

🏛️ Museums

26Alekos Fassianos Museum

🏛️ Museums● Classicfrom Erhan
Neofytou Metaxa 15, 104 39 Athens · Agios Pavlos (near Larissa Station / Metaxourgeio) · ~2.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

The painter Alekos Fassianos's family home, an apartment block he remodelled with architect Kyriakos Krokos over two decades (finished 1996); opened as a museum to his work in 2023.

🖼️ Galleries & art spaces

28The Breeder

🖼️ Galleries & art spaces● Alternativefrom Erhan
Iasonos 45, 104 36 Athens · Metaxourgeio · ~3.3 km (metro/taxi) from base

Leading contemporary art gallery founded in 2002 by Stathis Panagoulis and George Vamvakidis, growing out of The Breeder magazine. It is in a converted 1970s ice-cream factory and shows Greek and international artists at major fairs.

Kolonaki & Lycabettus

54Lycabettus Hill walk

⛰️ Walks, views & activities● Must-seefrom Giulia
Lycabettus Hill (paths from Kolonaki, e.g. top of Aristippou / Ploutarchou) · Kolonaki / Lycabettus · ~3.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

At 277 m, the highest hill in central Athens, with the chapel of Agios Georgios on top and 360° views over the city to the sea. You can walk up through pines in about 20-30 minutes, or take the funicular from Aristippou St.

Acropolis, Koukaki, Thissio & Petralona

70National Observatory of Athens - Thissio Visitor Center (evening tour)

⛰️ Walks, views & activities● Classicfrom Giulia
Hill of the Nymphs (Lofos Nymfon), Thissio, Athens 118 51 · Thissio / Hill of the Nymphs · ~4.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

The historic 1842 observatory on the Hill of the Nymphs runs evening visits with the Geoastrophysics museum, the historic Synge/Doridis telescopes and stargazing (weather permitting).

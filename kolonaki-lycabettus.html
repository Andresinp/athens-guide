Kolonaki & Lycabettus

The poshest district: museums, galleries, boutiques and smart cafés, with Lycabettus hill rising above.

Vibe key: ● Alternative · ● Trendy · ● Classic · ● Posh · ● Must-see

🗺️ Neighbourhoods

40Kolonaki

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Poshfrom Irene
Kolonaki, Athens 106 73-106 75 · Kolonaki · ~4.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

The upscale district on the lower slopes of Lycabettus, with designer boutiques, galleries, embassies and people-watching cafés around Kolonaki Square (Plateia Filikis Etaireias). The poshest neighbourhood in central Athens: establishment and chic, the opposite of alternative.

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants

41Filippou (Taverna Filippou)

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classicfrom Giulia
Xenokratous 19, Athens 106 75 · Kolonaki · ~3.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

A third-generation family taverna founded in 1923 as a basement wine shop, at its current ground-floor site since 1968. It serves classic home-style Greek cooking from an open kitchen.

☕ Cafés & brunch● Classicfrom Erhan
Lykavittos footpath (Monopati Lykavittou), 114 71 Athens · Lycabettus Hill (above Kolonaki) · ~3.7 km (metro/taxi) from base

Café, concept store (fashion, homeware) and event space across three floors of a restored 1930s neoclassical townhouse that once belonged to collector Evripidis Koutlidis; farm-to-table brunch and bowls.

44Dexameni (café-ouzeri)

☕ Cafés & brunch● Classicfrom Irene
Plateia Dexamenis, Kolonaki, Athens 106 73 · Kolonaki · ~3.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

A historic open-air café and meze spot under the trees of Dexameni Square, named after Hadrian's 2nd-century reservoir. It was a haunt of writers like Papadiamantis and Varnalis, and the square also has a summer open-air cinema (Cine Dexameni).

45Queen Bee

☕ Cafés & brunch● Poshfrom Erhan
Patriarchou Ioakim 45, 106 76 Athens · Kolonaki · ~4.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

All-day bakehouse and brunch spot from Panagiotis Kalyvas (of Capanna and Kolonaki Kalamaki), known for sourdough and croissants such as a pistachio-praline one; busy with stylish Kolonaki locals.

🏛️ Museums● Classicfrom Giulia
Kriezotou 3, Athens 106 71 · Kolonaki / Syntagma · ~3.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

⛰️ Walks, views & activities

54Lycabettus Hill walk

⛰️ Walks, views & activities● Must-seefrom Giulia
Lycabettus Hill (paths from Kolonaki, e.g. top of Aristippou / Ploutarchou) · Kolonaki / Lycabettus · ~3.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

At 277 m, the highest hill in central Athens, with the chapel of Agios Georgios on top and 360° views over the city to the sea. You can walk up through pines in about 20-30 minutes, or take the funicular from Aristippou St.

Natural-wine bar and restaurant run by two Frenchmen (Rafael Wallon-Brownstone, Thomas Brengou) with Greek chef Stavros Chrysafidis; French-Greek small plates and a low-intervention list. No reservations, 70s-chic interior.

8Booze Cooperativa (K.O.T.E.S.)

🍸 Bars: cocktails & wine● Alternativefrom Giulia
Kolokotroni 57, Athens 105 60 · Monastiraki / Historic triangle · ~3.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

An alternative arts café-bar, founded in 1989 in a neoclassical building, with exhibitions, gigs, talks and a bohemian crowd. Since 2018 it has also been the headquarters of K.O.T.E.S., a tongue-in-cheek 'smokers' party' founded by Nikos Louvros.

Gazi, Kerameikos & Metaxourgeio

28The Breeder

🖼️ Galleries & art spaces● Alternativefrom Erhan
Iasonos 45, 104 36 Athens · Metaxourgeio · ~3.3 km (metro/taxi) from base

Leading contemporary art gallery founded in 2002 by Stathis Panagoulis and George Vamvakidis, growing out of The Breeder magazine. It is in a converted 1970s ice-cream factory and shows Greek and international artists at major fairs.

Exarchia, Omonia & north centre

38Ninetto Gallery

🖼️ Galleries & art spaces● Alternativefrom Erhan
Leof. Alexandras 2, 106 82 Athens · Pedion tou Areos / Exarchia edge (near the National Archaeological Museum) · ~38 min walk from base

Contemporary gallery co-founded by Romain Bitton and Fred de Schonen in a former apartment, so a visit feels like a collector's home; it shows international and Greece-based artists, often their first exhibitions.

39Romantso

🖼️ Galleries & art spaces● Alternativefrom Giulia
Anaxagora 3-5, Athens 105 52 · Omonia / Psyrri edge · ~3.4 km (metro/taxi) from base

A creative hub in the former printing works of 'Romantso' magazine. It combines a start-up incubator, artist studios and residencies with a café-bar and a busy programme of exhibitions, parties and concerts.

Kolonaki & Lycabettus

51Eleftheria Tseliou Gallery

🖼️ Galleries & art spaces● Poshfrom Erhan
Iraklitou 3, 106 73 Athens · Kolonaki · ~3.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

Contemporary art gallery founded in 2013 that represents about 15 established and emerging, mostly Greek artists, with around seven solo shows a year.

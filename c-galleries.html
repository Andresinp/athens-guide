Gazi, Kerameikos & Metaxourgeio

🖼️ 26The Breeder ★ 4.3 (159 Google reviews)

🖼️ Galleries & art spaces● Alternativefrom Erhan
Iasonos 45, 104 36 Athens · ~3.3 km (metro/taxi) from base
Reviewers mention: modern art gallery, space.

Leading contemporary art gallery founded in 2002 by Stathis Panagoulis and George Vamvakidis, growing out of The Breeder magazine. It is in a converted 1970s ice-cream factory and shows Greek and international artists at major fairs.

An alternative arts café-bar, founded in 1989 in a neoclassical building, with exhibitions, gigs, talks and a bohemian crowd. Since 2018 it has also been the headquarters of K.O.T.E.S., a tongue-in-cheek 'smokers' party' founded by Nikos Louvros.

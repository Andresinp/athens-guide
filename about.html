How this was made

The recommendations came from three friends: Erhan's Google Maps pins, Irene's list of restaurants, bars, clubs and neighbourhoods, and Giulia's museums, walks and links.

  • Erhan's 31 short links were resolved to their Google places. On this site they link to the original pins.
  • Places that came as plain names were identified on the web. They link to a Google Maps search for their exact name and address.
  • Every place's Google Maps listing was checked on 7 Oct 2026 for its category, rating, per-person price range, open/closed status and the topics reviewers mention.
  • "In practice" lines and item prices (cocktail, beer, wine, plate, coffee) come from reviews and menus (venue sites, Wolt/efood, TripAdvisor, press), each linked. Prices nobody published are left blank, not guessed.
  • Coordinates were cross-checked against OpenStreetMap and Google. Area shading is approximate.
  • Events were researched on 6 Oct 2026. Each carries its source and a confidence flag.

Permanently closed: skip these

Recommended, but Google Maps lists them as permanently closed (checked 7 Oct 2026).

  • Boiler (Vlachava 9, Athens 105 51): An underground bar on two levels, with a street-level bar and a cave-like basement for electronic, post-punk and experimental DJ and live sets. Firmly on the alternative scene.
  • Six d.o.g.s (Avramiotou 6-8, Athens 105 51): A bar, garden and live-music/arts venue opened in 2010 in a former carpentry workshop, with a leafy courtyard around an old tree. Coffee by day, cocktails and concerts or DJ nights after dark. Since April 2026 the old Six d.o.g.s site is run as Klakaz (cocktails €11–13, per Protothema, June 2026).

The official site lists only Madrid (plus links to Brooklyn and Santorini), and searches in English and Greek found no Athens shop. Probably a general recommendation, or a pointer to the people below.
  • Terry: Probably a person, not a venue: Terry Craven is a co-founder and co-owner of Desperate Literature (Madrid). UNVERIFIED / AMBIGUOUS. No Athens bar, café or bookshop called 'Terry' was found. Because it appears right after 'Desperate literature' in Irene's list, it most likely means Terry Craven, perhaps a friend to contact.   • Oli: Probably a person, not a venue: Desperate Literature's 'Who we are' page lists a staff member named Ollie Edwards. UNVERIFIED / AMBIGUOUS. No Athens venue called 'Oli' was found (search turned up only unrelated places such as Olive in Glyfada and Olio Cafe in Gerakas). Likely refers to Ollie of Desperate Literature. Ask Irene.
  • Los días perfectos - Jacobo Bergareche (book): Jacobo Bergareche's 2021 debut novel (Libros del Asteroide): in Austin, a married man finds William Faulkner's letters to his lover Meta Carpenter and writes two long letters weighing passion against marital tedium. Book recommendation only. Leave it off the map.

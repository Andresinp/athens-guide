How this was made

The recommendations came from three friends: Erhan's Google Maps pins, Irene's list of restaurants, bars, clubs and neighbourhoods, and Giulia's museums, walks and links.

  • Erhan's 31 short links were resolved to their Google places. On this site they link to the original pins.
  • Places that came as plain names were identified on the web. They link to a Google Maps search for their exact name and address.
  • Every pin's coordinates were cross-checked against OpenStreetMap, reverse-geocoded, and compared to the street address.
  • Places that couldn't be confirmed carry a ⚠ note. Nothing was filled in by guesswork.
  • Events were researched on 6 Oct 2026. Each carries its source and a confidence flag.

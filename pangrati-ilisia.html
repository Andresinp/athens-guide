Pangrati, Mets & Ilisia

Residential, local and foodie, east of the Panathenaic Stadium. Many of Athens' best new restaurants are here.

🏘️ 53Pangrati

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Classicfrom Irene
Pangrati, Athens 116 35

A residential district behind the Panathenaic Stadium, centred on Plateia Varnava and Plateia Plastira, with a strong local food and bar scene and few tourists. Middle-class and quietly hip: more neighbourhood-bohemian than either alternative or posh.

It is on Michelin and 50 Best Discovery lists.

A bistronomy restaurant and bakery with an open kitchen, a counter and a wood-fired grill. It runs as a bakery-café for breakfast, with dinner as the main event. Chefs Giannis Loukakis and Spiros Pediaditakis opened it in 2023, and it holds a Michelin Bib Gourmand (since the 2024 Athens guide).

Gazi, Kerameikos & Metaxourgeio

26Alekos Fassianos Museum

🏛️ Museums● Classicfrom Erhan
Neofytou Metaxa 15, 104 39 Athens · Agios Pavlos (near Larissa Station / Metaxourgeio) · ~2.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

The painter Alekos Fassianos's family home, an apartment block he remodelled with architect Kyriakos Krokos over two decades (finished 1996); opened as a museum to his work in 2023.

🏛️ Museums● Alternativefrom Giulia
Pireos 138 & Andronikou, Athens 118 54 · Rouf / Gazi · ~4.7 km (metro/taxi) from base

Exarchia, Omonia & north centre

37National Archaeological Museum

🏛️ Museums● Must-seefrom Giulia
28is Oktovriou (Patision) 44, Athens 106 82 · Exarcheia / Mouseio · ~2.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

Greece's largest archaeological museum, with prehistoric to late-antique finds: the Mask of Agamemnon, the Artemision Bronze and the Antikythera Mechanism.

Kolonaki & Lycabettus

47Benaki Museum - Nikos Hadjikyriakos-Ghika Gallery

🏛️ Museums● Classicfrom Giulia
Kriezotou 3, Athens 106 71 · Kolonaki / Syntagma · ~3.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

The former home and studio of modernist painter Nikos Hadjikyriakos-Ghika (1906-94), now a Benaki branch showing his work and that of his circle, with views from the upper floors.

48Benaki Museum (main building)

🏛️ Museums● Must-seefrom Erhan & Giulia
Koumpari 1, 106 74 Athens · Kolonaki (opposite the National Garden) · ~4.1 km (metro/taxi) from base

The Benaki's flagship mansion, covering Greek art and culture from prehistory to the 20th century; its rooftop café looks over the National Garden.

49Museum of Cycladic Art

🏛️ Museums● Must-seefrom Erhan & Giulia
Neofytou Douka 4, 106 74 Athens · Kolonaki · ~4.2 km (metro/taxi) from base

Museum built around the Goulandris collection of Cycladic figurines and ancient Greek and Cypriot art, also staging major contemporary art shows.

50Byzantine & Christian Museum

🏛️ Museums● Classicfrom Giulia
Vasilissis Sofias 22, Athens 106 75 · Kolonaki / Evangelismos · ~4.3 km (metro/taxi) from base

A national museum of Byzantine and post-Byzantine art (icons, mosaics, frescoes, manuscripts) in the 1840s Villa Ilissia, built for the Duchess of Plaisance, with a large modern underground extension and gardens.

Acropolis, Koukaki, Thissio & Petralona

68Benaki Museum of Islamic Art

🏛️ Museums● Classicfrom Giulia
Agion Asomaton 22 & Dipylou 12, Athens 105 53 · Kerameikos / Thissio · ~3.8 km (metro/taxi) from base

The Benaki's Islamic collection (ceramics, metalwork, textiles, carved woodwork from the 7th-19th c.) in two neoclassical mansions. Part of the ancient city wall is visible in the basement, and there is a rooftop café facing Kerameikos.

Open in Google Maps ↗Website / IG ↗Area: Acropolis, Koukaki, Thissio & Petralona

69Acropolis Museum

🏛️ Museums● Must-seefrom Erhan & Giulia
Dionysiou Areopagitou 15, 117 42 Athens · Makrygianni (below the Acropolis) · ~4.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

Bernard Tschumi-designed museum (opened 2009) for finds from the Acropolis, including the Parthenon gallery; glass floors show the excavations below.

Psyri & Monastiraki

3Ta Karamanlidika tou Fani

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classicfrom Irene
Sokratous 1 & Evripidou, Athens 105 52 (deli/pastirma shop at Evripidou 41) · Psyrri / Central Market (Varvakeios) area · ~3.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

A meze restaurant and deli built on the cured-meat traditions of the Karamanlides, Anatolian Greek-Orthodox refugees: pastirma, soutzouki and cheeses. Fanis Theodoropoulos continues his family's pastirma trade (1920s), and the restaurant has occupied the corner neoclassical building since 2014.

Exarchia, Omonia & north centre

29Pharaoh

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Alternativefrom Erhan
Solomou 54, 106 82 Athens · Exarchia · ~2.8 km (metro/taxi) from base

Restaurant and natural-wine bar where chef Manolis Papoutsakis cooks Greek dishes without electricity, using wood stoves, charcoal and a wood oven; vinyl DJ in the evenings, behind a heavy curtain in edgy Exarchia.

30I Kriti (Η Κρήτη)

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classicfrom Erhan & Giulia
Veranzerou 5, 106 77 Athens · Omonia (Kaningos Square) · ~3.1 km (metro/taxi) from base

Family-run Cretan taverna hidden in an arcade (stoa) off Veranzerou, around for nearly three decades and now seen as a classic. It uses produce from Cretan cooperatives: apaki, Sfakian pies, liver with honey.

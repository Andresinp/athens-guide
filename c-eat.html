Psyri & Monastiraki

🍽️ 3Ta Karamanlidika tou Fani ★ 4.7 (14,334 Google reviews)

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classic€€from Irene
Sokratous 1 & Evripidou, Athens 105 52 (deli/pastirma shop at Evripidou 41)
💶 €€ · €15–25 pp (Google)
🍺 beer €4.5 · 🍷 wine €4–6.50 · 🍽️ plate €6.50–8.50 source ↗ source 2 ↗

A deli and charcuterie shop with a busy meze restaurant attached, serving pastirma, soutzouki, cured meats, cheeses and Anatolian-Greek small plates with ouzo, tsipouro and wine. It is built on the cured-meat traditions of the Karamanlides, Anatolian Greek-Orthodox refugees. Fanis Theodoropoulos continues his family's 1920s pastirma trade, and the restaurant has occupied its corner neoclassical building since 2014.

Psyri & Monastiraki

1Psiri (Psyrri)

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Alternativefrom Irene
Psyrri, Athens 105 54 · Psyrri · ~3.8 km (metro/taxi) from base

A former craftsmen's quarter next to Monastiraki, now packed with tavernas, bars, street art and late-night music. It is fairly alternative and gritty-bohemian (but busy with visitors), with no poshness at all.

2Monastiraki

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Must-seefrom Irene
Monastiraki, Athens 105 55 · Monastiraki · ~4.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

The busy square, metro hub and flea market below the Acropolis, full of souvenir and vintage stalls and rooftop bars with views. Lively and mainstream-touristy, with a slightly scruffy edge. Not posh.

Plaka, Syntagma & historic centre

15Plaka

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Must-seefrom Irene
Plaka, Athens 105 58 · Plaka · ~4.4 km (metro/taxi) from base

The oldest quarter, right under the Acropolis: pedestrian lanes, neoclassical houses and the Cycladic-style Anafiotika hamlet. Pretty and very touristy, neither alternative nor posh (tourist-priced tavernas).

Kolonaki & Lycabettus

40Kolonaki

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Poshfrom Irene
Kolonaki, Athens 106 73-106 75 · Kolonaki · ~4.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

The upscale district on the lower slopes of Lycabettus, with designer boutiques, galleries, embassies and people-watching cafés around Kolonaki Square (Plateia Filikis Etaireias). The poshest neighbourhood in central Athens: establishment and chic, the opposite of alternative.

Pangrati, Mets & Ilisia

55Pangrati

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Classicfrom Irene
Pangrati, Athens 116 35 · Pangrati · ~5.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

A residential district behind the Panathenaic Stadium, centred on Plateia Varnava and Plateia Plastira, with a strong local food and bar scene and few tourists. Middle-class and quietly hip: more neighbourhood-bohemian than either alternative or posh.

Acropolis, Koukaki, Thissio & Petralona

61Thissio

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Must-seefrom Irene
Thissio, Athens 118 51 · Thissio · ~4.2 km (metro/taxi) from base

The area around the pedestrian Apostolou Pavlou promenade, with cafés facing the Acropolis and the Temple of Hephaestus in the Ancient Agora. Relaxed and somewhat touristy but loved by Athenians for evening strolls: mid-range, neither posh nor very alternative.

62Filopappou Hill (Philopappos)

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Must-seefrom Irene & Giulia
Filopappou Hill, Athens 117 41 · Filopappou / Ano Petralona / Koukaki · ~5.1 km (metro/taxi) from base

A pine-covered archaeological hill south-west of the Acropolis, topped by the 2nd-century Philopappos Monument and crossed by Pikionis' stone paths. It has the classic Acropolis and sunset view, is free and open, and has a local, unpretentious feel, more green space than scene.

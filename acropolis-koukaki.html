Acropolis, Koukaki, Thissio & Petralona

The ancient core and the green hills (Filopappou, Nymphs). Below them are relaxed neighbourhoods with old tavernas.

🏘️ Neighbourhoods

🏘️ 59Thissio

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Must-seefrom Irene
Thissio, Athens 118 51 · ~4.2 km (metro/taxi) from base

The area around the pedestrian Apostolou Pavlou promenade, with cafés facing the Acropolis and the Temple of Hephaestus in the Ancient Agora. Relaxed and somewhat touristy but loved by Athenians for evening strolls: mid-range, neither posh nor very alternative.

🏘️ 60Filopappou Hill (Philopappos)

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Must-seefrom Irene & Giulia
Filopappou Hill, Athens 117 41 · ~5.1 km (metro/taxi) from base

A pine-covered archaeological hill south-west of the Acropolis, topped by the 2nd-century Philopappos Monument and crossed by Pikionis' stone paths. It has the classic Acropolis and sunset view, is free and open, and has a local, unpretentious feel, more green space than scene.

🏛️ 66Benaki Museum of Islamic Art

🏛️ Museums● Classicfrom Giulia
Agion Asomaton 22 & Dipylou 12, Athens 105 53 · ~3.8 km (metro/taxi) from base
⏸ Temporarily closed on Google (checked 7 Oct 2026). Check before going.

The Benaki's Islamic collection (ceramics, metalwork, textiles, carved woodwork from the 7th-19th c.) in two neoclassical mansions. Part of the ancient city wall is visible in the basement, and there is a rooftop café facing Kerameikos.

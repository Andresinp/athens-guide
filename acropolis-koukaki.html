Acropolis, Koukaki, Thissio & Petralona

The ancient core and the green hills (Filopappou, Nymphs). Below them are relaxed neighbourhoods with old tavernas.

Vibe key: ● Alternative · ● Trendy · ● Classic · ● Posh · ● Must-see

🗺️ Neighbourhoods

61Thissio

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Must-seefrom Irene
Thissio, Athens 118 51 · Thissio · ~4.2 km (metro/taxi) from base

The area around the pedestrian Apostolou Pavlou promenade, with cafés facing the Acropolis and the Temple of Hephaestus in the Ancient Agora. Relaxed and somewhat touristy but loved by Athenians for evening strolls: mid-range, neither posh nor very alternative.

62Filopappou Hill (Philopappos)

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Must-seefrom Irene & Giulia
Filopappou Hill, Athens 117 41 · Filopappou / Ano Petralona / Koukaki · ~5.1 km (metro/taxi) from base

A pine-covered archaeological hill south-west of the Acropolis, topped by the 2nd-century Philopappos Monument and crossed by Pikionis' stone paths. It has the classic Acropolis and sunset view, is free and open, and has a local, unpretentious feel, more green space than scene.

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants

63Taverna Oikonomou

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classicfrom Giulia
Troon 41 & Kydantidon 32, Athens 118 51 · Ano Petralona · ~5.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

An old-school Petralona cook-house, operating since the 1930s by several accounts, serving home-style Greek dishes at a neighbourhood taverna with tables outside.

64Phita (Fita / ΦΙΤΑ)

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Trendyfrom Irene
Ntourm 1, Athens 117 45 (some listings: Beka 1) · Neos Kosmos · ~5.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

A modern Greek 'gastro-taverna' opened in 2019 by Fotis Foteinoglou and Theodoros Kassavetis, with a daily-changing menu of seafood, vegetables and pulses from small producers. It is credited with putting Neos Kosmos on the food map.

🏛️ Museums

68Benaki Museum of Islamic Art

🏛️ Museums● Classicfrom Giulia
Agion Asomaton 22 & Dipylou 12, Athens 105 53 · Kerameikos / Thissio · ~3.8 km (metro/taxi) from base

The Benaki's Islamic collection (ceramics, metalwork, textiles, carved woodwork from the 7th-19th c.) in two neoclassical mansions. Part of the ancient city wall is visible in the basement, and there is a rooftop café facing Kerameikos.

69Acropolis Museum

🏛️ Museums● Must-seefrom Erhan & Giulia
Dionysiou Areopagitou 15, 117 42 Athens · Makrygianni (below the Acropolis) · ~4.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

Bernard Tschumi-designed museum (opened 2009) for finds from the Acropolis, including the Parthenon gallery; glass floors show the excavations below.

70National Observatory of Athens - Thissio Visitor Center (evening tour)

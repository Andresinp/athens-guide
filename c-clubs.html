Plaka, Syntagma & historic centre

20Dybbuk

🪩 Clubs & late night● Alternativefrom Irene
Stadiou 7 (arcade), Athens 105 62 (latest known address) · Syntagma · ~4.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

A house-music club that opened around 2009 in a Kolonaki basement (Patriarchou Ioakeim 37) and later moved to the Stadiou 7 arcade near Syntagma. It was known for big production nights and winter-only seasons.

⚠ POSSIBLY CLOSED. Resident Advisor lists 'Dybbuk (closed)' and Foursquare marks both the Kolonaki and Stadiou 7 venues 'Now Closed'. However, RA shows a Dybbuk event in early 2025 and some sites still list a New Year's Eve party. Check its social media before recommending.

Gazi, Kerameikos & Metaxourgeio

23SMUT Athens

🪩 Clubs & late night● Alternativefrom Erhan & Giulia
Vatsaxi 4, 104 38 Athens · Metaxourgeio / Plateia Vathis · ~2.8 km (metro/taxi) from base

Identified: Vatsaxi 4 is SMUT, an underground queer techno club open on Saturdays. It is known for its safe-space and no-camera policies and a raw, red-lit, Berlin-warehouse look.

🪩 Clubs & late night● Trendyfrom Erhan

Exarchia, Omonia & north centre

35Skullbar

🪩 Clubs & late night● Alternativefrom Irene
Lamprou Katsoni 11-13 & Ippokratous, Athens 114 72 · Gyzi / upper Neapoli (Exarcheia side) · ~3.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

An underground bar and club that is an alternative café by day and a dance club by night. It is best known for weekend after-hours parties (roughly 05:00 to midday) with techno and house.

36Don't be a D!ck

🪩 Clubs & late night● Alternativefrom Erhan
Fidiou 4, 106 78 Athens · Exarchia (near Panepistimiou) · ~3.3 km (metro/taxi) from base

Fairly new Exarchia bar with a 70s–80s Fiorucci-style look, 48-hour sourdough pizzas from a wood-fired oven, and DJ nights (italo and space disco, funk); its downstairs 'Toilet Room' has pink tiles and a disco ball.

Kolonaki & Lycabettus

46Rock'n'Roll

🪩 Clubs & late night● Poshfrom Irene
Loukianou 6, Athens 106 75 · Kolonaki · ~4.2 km (metro/taxi) from base

A Kolonaki institution since 1987: a bar-restaurant by day and early evening (pizza, burgers), then a crowded club with DJs and party nights. Its crowd is the smart Kolonaki set.

Psyri & Monastiraki

Flea-market lanes, street art, tavernas and bars packed into the old commercial centre.

Vibe key: ● Alternative · ● Trendy · ● Classic · ● Posh · ● Must-see

🗺️ Neighbourhoods

1Psiri (Psyrri)

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Alternativefrom Irene
Psyrri, Athens 105 54 · Psyrri · ~3.8 km (metro/taxi) from base

A former craftsmen's quarter next to Monastiraki, now packed with tavernas, bars, street art and late-night music. It is fairly alternative and gritty-bohemian (but busy with visitors), with no poshness at all.

2Monastiraki

🗺️ Neighbourhoods● Must-seefrom Irene
Monastiraki, Athens 105 55 · Monastiraki · ~4.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

The busy square, metro hub and flea market below the Acropolis, full of souvenir and vintage stalls and rooftop bars with views. Lively and mainstream-touristy, with a slightly scruffy edge. Not posh.

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants

3Ta Karamanlidika tou Fani

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classicfrom Irene
Sokratous 1 & Evripidou, Athens 105 52 (deli/pastirma shop at Evripidou 41) · Psyrri / Central Market (Varvakeios) area · ~3.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

A meze restaurant and deli built on the cured-meat traditions of the Karamanlides, Anatolian Greek-Orthodox refugees: pastirma, soutzouki and cheeses. Fanis Theodoropoulos continues his family's pastirma trade (1920s), and the restaurant has occupied the corner neoclassical building since 2014.

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Trendyfrom Giulia

A restaurant, shop, natural-candle maker and creative office in one bright corner space, opened Dec 2021 by Myrsini (Linou) and George (Soumpasis). It also hosts exhibitions and record launches.

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classicfrom Erhan

Traditional Greek restaurant opened in 1986 by Ketty Koufonikola on the flea-market square; antique-cluttered interior, Acropolis-view terrace, and live folk music at weekends from September to June.

🍸 Bars: cocktails & wine● Alternativefrom Giulia
Kolokotroni 57, Athens 105 60 · Monastiraki / Historic triangle · ~3.9 km (metro/taxi) from base

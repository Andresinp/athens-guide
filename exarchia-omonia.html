Exarchia, Omonia & north centre

Student, anarchist and literary Athens. It's gritty and political, full of cheap ouzeris and record and book shops.

Vibe key: ● Alternative · ● Trendy · ● Classic · ● Posh · ● Must-see

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants

29Pharaoh

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Alternativefrom Erhan
Solomou 54, 106 82 Athens · Exarchia · ~2.8 km (metro/taxi) from base

Restaurant and natural-wine bar where chef Manolis Papoutsakis cooks Greek dishes without electricity, using wood stoves, charcoal and a wood oven; vinyl DJ in the evenings, behind a heavy curtain in edgy Exarchia.

30I Kriti (Η Κρήτη)

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classicfrom Erhan & Giulia
Veranzerou 5, 106 77 Athens · Omonia (Kaningos Square) · ~3.1 km (metro/taxi) from base

Family-run Cretan taverna hidden in an arcade (stoa) off Veranzerou, around for nearly three decades and now seen as a classic. It uses produce from Cretan cooperatives: apaki, Sfakian pies, liver with honey.

31Ouzeri Lesvos

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classicfrom Erhan
Emmanouil Benaki 38, 106 78 Athens · Exarchia · ~3.1 km (metro/taxi) from base

Long-standing Exarchia ouzeri serving seafood and meze from Lesvos or cooked the Lesvos way, at modest prices; a local favourite.

32Zigoala (Ζιγκοάλα)

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Trendyfrom Erhan
Lykourgou 9, 105 51 Athens · Omonia · ~3.4 km (metro/taxi) from base

Modern taverna by chef Vasilis Chammam serving meze with Eastern influences; widely reviewed (Athinorama, Lifo, FNL) and credited with drawing food lovers to Omonia.

33Diporto

🍽️ Eat: tavernas & restaurants● Classicfrom Erhan
Sokratous 9 & Theatrou 2, 105 52 Athens · Central Market (Varvakeios) / Psyri–Omonia · ~3.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

Signless basement taverna under an old neoclassical building by the central market. There is no menu: a handful of daily dishes (chickpeas, stews, salad) and retsina from the barrel. Open daytime only.

🪩 Clubs & late night

35Skullbar

🪩 Clubs & late night● Alternativefrom Irene
Lamprou Katsoni 11-13 & Ippokratous, Athens 114 72 · Gyzi / upper Neapoli (Exarcheia side) · ~3.0 km (metro/taxi) from base

An underground bar and club that is an alternative café by day and a dance club by night. It is best known for weekend after-hours parties (roughly 05:00 to midday) with techno and house.

🏛️ Museums

37National Archaeological Museum

🏛️ Museums● Must-seefrom Giulia
28is Oktovriou (Patision) 44, Athens 106 82 · Exarcheia / Mouseio · ~2.6 km (metro/taxi) from base

Greece's largest archaeological museum, with prehistoric to late-antique finds: the Mask of Agamemnon, the Artemision Bronze and the Antikythera Mechanism.

38Ninetto Gallery

🖼️ Galleries & art spaces● Alternativefrom Erhan
Leof. Alexandras 2, 106 82 Athens · Pedion tou Areos / Exarchia edge (near the National Archaeological Museum) · ~38 min walk from base

Contemporary gallery co-founded by Romain Bitton and Fred de Schonen in a former apartment, so a visit feels like a collector's home; it shows international and Greece-based artists, often their first exhibitions.

39Romantso

🖼️ Galleries & art spaces● Alternativefrom Giulia
Anaxagora 3-5, Athens 105 52 · Omonia / Psyrri edge · ~3.4 km (metro/taxi) from base

A creative hub in the former printing works of 'Romantso' magazine. It combines a start-up incubator, artist studios and residencies with a café-bar and a busy programme of exhibitions, parties and concerts.
